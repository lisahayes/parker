Snaking vs. hydro jetting in Parker, CO

Drain snaking clears most kitchen, bathroom, and single fixture clogs. A flexible cable runs down the line to break up or retrieve the blockage. It is fast and affordable for a single stubborn clog.

Hydro jetting does a more thorough job on grease heavy kitchen lines or a drain that clogs repeatedly. High pressure water scours the full interior of the pipe rather than just punching through the clog, which is why jetted lines tend to stay clear longer than snaked ones.

Mainline calls

When more than one drain is slow

When several fixtures back up at once, the problem is rarely any single drain. It is the mainline or sewer lateral leaving the house. We run a sewer camera before recommending a fix on mainline calls, because a mainline clog and a mainline leak look identical from inside the house but need completely different repairs. If the camera shows a genuine leak rather than a clearable blockage, see our sewer line leak page instead.

Drain Cleaning questions Parker calls in with

Snaking or hydro jetting: which do I need?

Snaking is faster and cheaper for a single stubborn clog. Jetting costs more but scours the full pipe wall and lasts longer, worth it for grease heavy kitchen lines or repeat clogs.

Multiple drains are slow at once. Is that one problem?

Almost always. The mainline leaving the house rather than each fixture individually, which calls for a camera inspection instead of snaking each drain separately.
