Gas Leak Detection & Repair in Parker, CO

A suspected gas leak is not a wait-and-see problem. Xcel Energy handles the utility side, and we locate and repair the line itself, any hour of the day.

Xcel Energy serves natural gas throughout Parker and Douglas County. The utility responds to reported leaks and can shut off service, but locating the exact failure point and completing a repair is licensed plumbing work. The steel gas lines running to Parker's older Pinery homes are often the same age as the copper supply lines now developing pinhole leaks throughout the same neighborhoods. Decades in the ground and appliance connectors that outlived their rated service life go unnoticed until something gives.

Unlike a pinhole leak, a gas leak rarely leaves a slow water bill trail to reveal it. Usually it is a faint rotten egg smell near a meter or appliance, a hissing sound, or in bad cases a dead patch of grass over a buried line. That smell is a signal to leave the house immediately, not to investigate room by room. Call (303) 552-3896 once you are safely outside.

First steps

What to do first if you smell gas in Parker, CO

Leave the house immediately. Do not flip a light switch, use a phone indoors, or search for the source with a lighter. A faint smell can concentrate quickly in an enclosed space.

Shut off the meter if you can reach it without lingering. A quarter turn with a wrench so the valve sits crosswise to the pipe stops flow at the source.

Call Xcel Energy from outside, then call us. Only Xcel Energy can restore service to the meter once a repair is complete, which is why we coordinate with them directly rather than around them.

Repair scope

Common gas line failure points in Parker, CO

Flexible appliance connectors. The corrugated line to a stove, dryer, or water heater has a service life. A common failure point is a fixture swapped with the old connector reused.

Meter sets on older homes. Thread sealant used decades ago in Pinery-era construction was sometimes never rated for the pressure now running through it.

Buried steel service lines. Corrosion in the ground shows up as a smell in the yard or dead grass in a straight line. Once Xcel confirms it is safe to proceed, we pressure test the line in sections and use a combustible gas detector along every joint and connection, tracing buried yard lines with the same acoustic methods used on buried water leaks.

Repairs beyond a simple connector swap are permitted work, and we pull those permits so the fix is documented and inspected.

Gas Leak Detection & Repair questions Parker calls in with

Is it safe to stay inside if I smell a little gas?

No. Leave immediately and call from outside. A faint smell can concentrate quickly in an enclosed space, and there is no reliable way to judge severity by nose alone.

Will Xcel Energy find the leak for me?

Xcel Energy responds to reported leaks and can shut off service if needed, but locating the exact failure point and completing the repair is licensed plumbing work, which is where we come in.

How much does gas leak repair cost in Parker?

It depends entirely on where the leak is. An appliance connector swap is usually a same visit fix. A buried service line replacement is a bigger job. We quote in writing after locating the leak, before repair starts.
